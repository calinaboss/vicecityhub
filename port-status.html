GTA 6 PC Port Status

Reverse engineering and native recompilation tracker following the leaked debug executable drop.

Leaked on November 23, 2026 • 14:36 UTC • Elapsed: 00d 00h 00m 00s

Current State

Verified functionality across tested community builds.

What Works

  • Executable boots directly into game with full character control and HUD
  • Physics engine, collision detection, and vehicular handling functional
  • DLSS and Frame Generation integration working on RTX 40/50 GPUs
  • In-engine audio, dialogue playback, and vehicle radio stations active
  • Prologue and opening story missions verified playable without crashes

What's Blocking & In Progress

  • Ray tracing global illumination causes severe FPS drops unless disabled
  • Initial shader compilation stutter across non-NVIDIA GPUs
  • VRAM memory leak after 45+ minutes of continuous gameplay
  • Take-Two & Rockstar DMCA notices against public GitHub forks
