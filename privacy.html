Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • GDPR & EU Compliance

1. Overview & Data Philosophy

At ViceCityHub.eu, we believe in radical privacy and data minimization. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines what minimal technical data is processed when you visit or participate in our gaming hub.

2. Information We Process

Technical Logs: When connecting to our website through Cloudflare edge servers, standard HTTP access logs (such as truncated IP address, user-agent string, and timestamp) are processed strictly for DDoS mitigation, bot defense, and web performance monitoring.

User Submissions: Content you publish voluntarily (including forum titles, comments, and submitted Discord server invitations) is publicly visible to all visitors. Do not post sensitive personal data or private phone numbers.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use invasive third-party cross-site tracking cookies. We utilize browser localStorage strictly to store your interface preferences (such as theme and bookmarked discussions). For more details, consult our Cookie Policy.

4. Cloudflare Infrastructure & Security

ViceCityHub.eu is hosted via Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare serves as a secure proxy and content delivery network, encrypting all traffic via TLS 1.3 and ensuring protection against automated attacks and OWASP vulnerabilities.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR (EU Residents)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users residing in the European Economic Area possess the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of any personally identifiable information associated with their activity.

6. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

For any data protection inquiries or removal requests, please reach out via our community discord or contact channel.
