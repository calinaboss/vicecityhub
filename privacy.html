2. Information We Collect & Process

  • Account & Authentication Data: If you register or sign in (via email/password or Google OAuth 2.0), we store your chosen username, email address, hashed credentials or OAuth identifier, and profile avatar.

4. European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom & Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, our processing of your personal data is governed by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation), the UK Data Protection Act 2018, and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

  • Legal Bases for Processing (Art. 6 GDPR): We rely on your explicit Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for analytics and advertising cookies via Google Consent Mode v2; Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)) to provide registered user accounts and community features; and Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for network security, anti-spam moderation, and fraud prevention.
  • Your European Privacy Rights: You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectify (Art. 16), erase / "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17), restrict processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and object to processing (Art. 21). You may withdraw your cookie consent at any time by clicking "Privacy & Cookie Settings" in the website footer. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national Data Protection Authority (DPA).

5. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, UCPA)

This section applies to residents of California (under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA), Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, and other US states with applicable consumer privacy laws.

  • Categories of Personal Information Collected: Identifiers (username, email address, IP address), Internet or other electronic network activity information (interaction with pages and advertisements), and user-submitted forum content.
  • Right to Know, Access & Delete: You have the right to request disclosure of the personal information we collect and to request the deletion of your account and personal information.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration. Under the CCPA/CPRA, you may opt out of personalized advertising cookies at any time via Google Ads Settings or via your browser's Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

6. Asia-Pacific (APAC), Canada (PIPEDA) & Brazil (LGPD) Compliance

  • Brazil (LGPD - Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, Law No. 13.709/2018): Brazilian residents have the right to confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion of data processed with consent, and revocation of consent under Art. 18 of the LGPD.
  • Canada (PIPEDA): Canadian residents may request access to and correction of their personal data and withdraw consent for non-essential advertising cookies at any time.
  • Japan (APPI), South Korea (PIPA), Singapore (PDPA), India (DPDP Act) & Australia (Privacy Act 1988): We adhere to international data minimization, purpose limitation, and transparency principles. Cross-border data transfers through our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare, Inc.) and Google LLC are safeguarded by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and recognized international data transfer frameworks.

ViceCityHub.eu is dedicated to Grand Theft Auto VI, a mature-rated video game title, and is intended for audiences aged 16 and older (or at least 13 years of age under US COPPA). 8. Data Retention & Security

All traffic is encrypted via TLS 1.3 through Cloudflare's global edge network. Account data and community posts are retained for as long as your account remains active or until you delete your content or request account deletion via your profile settings or by emailing us.

9. How to Exercise Your Rights or Contact Us

To exercise any of your global privacy rights, submit a data deletion request, or ask questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at legal@vicecityhub.eu or info@vicecityhub.eu, or use our Notice & Takedown / Contact Form. We respond to all verified privacy requests within 30 days.
