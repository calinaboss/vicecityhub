GTA VI Cheat Codes (PS5 & Xbox Series X/S)

The input method is identical on both consoles—only the shoulder button names change. All GTA 6 cheats are activated through the in-game phone and use only the D-pad.

PlayStation 5 Activation

Press Up on the D-pad to pull out your phone. Hold L1, press R1, then enter the D-pad sequence.

Xbox Series X/S Activation

Press Up on the D-pad to pull out your phone. Hold LB, press RB, then enter the D-pad sequence.

Our Tip: Watch Similar Codes

Some codes look almost identical. Attack Force, Exploding Balls, and Vehicle Force differ by only one or two inputs. Take your time—one wrong press will trigger a different cheat or nothing at all.

Memorize the Input Patterns

Cheats follow patterns! Vehicle spawns always start with Left, Up, Right, Down. Ammo cheats all share the same opening: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up. Learn the prefix and you only need to remember the final inputs.

Phone Numbers in GTA 6: Do They Exist?

Short answer: No. In GTA 4 and GTA 5 you dialed numbers on your phone. In GTA 6, the phone is still your gateway to cheats, but you don't dial anything—you use the D-pad combination. Any lists of 1-999 numbers online are just repackaged GTA 5 codes.
