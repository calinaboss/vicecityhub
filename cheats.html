GTA VI Cheat Codes (PS5 & Xbox Series X/S)

The input method is identical on both consoles—only the shoulder button names change. All GTA 6 cheats are activated through the in-game phone and use only the D-pad.

PlayStation 5 Activation

Press Up on the D-pad to pull out your phone. Hold L1, press R1, then enter the D-pad sequence.

Xbox Series X/S Activation

Press Up on the D-pad to pull out your phone. Hold LB, press RB, then enter the D-pad sequence.

How to Unlock the Cheats Menu

Cheats unlock after clearing the main story. Open your Map → Progression → Cheats tab. Each cheat has an in-game challenge (e.g., fenced goods, shooting ranges, or stunts), and your progress before rolling credits counts toward unlocking them!

Memorize the Input Patterns

Cheats follow patterns: Vehicle spawns start with Left, Up, Right, Down. Ammo cheats start with Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up. Screen Filters start with Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left.

Important: Saves & Achievements

Activating any cheat disables manual/auto saving and blocks Trophies/Achievements for the rest of your session. Always save your game before activating a cheat so you never lose story or challenge progress.
