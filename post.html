Posting Rules & Guidelines

To keep ViceCityHub clean, fast, and helpful for all players, every post must follow these mandatory rules:

  • 1. Be Respectful & Constructive

    No harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, or toxic behavior toward other community members.

  • 2. Tag Major Story Spoilers

    If your post discusses late-game missions or endings, include [SPOILER] at the beginning of your title.

  • 3. Include Coordinates for Map Finds

    When sharing hidden locations, safehouses, or easter eggs, reference the district or coordinates from map.vicecityhub.eu.

  • 4. No Scams, Malware, or Fake Generators

    Phishing links, fake money generators, or external executables are strictly prohibited and automatically blocked.

  • 5. Use the Dedicated Discord Directory

    Do not spam Discord invite links in the main feed. Use the Submit Discord Server page instead.
