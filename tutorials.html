How to Find Free Supercars & Secret Vehicles

Across Vice City and Port Gellhorn, several high-end exotics and tuned muscle cars spawn in unmarked garages and secluded alleyways. Keep an eye out for multi-level parking decks in Vice Beach where vehicles like the Grotti Cheetah, Pegassi Torero, and Pfister Comet can be hotwired without triggering an immediate police response.

Convenience Store Robberies & Safe Cracking

In Grand Theft Auto VI, dynamic store holdups allow dual protagonists Jason and Lucia to control cashiers, scout manager offices, and discover written safe combinations on bulletin boards. Timing your getaway through back alley exits is essential to break line of sight with Leonida Highway Patrol squad cars.
