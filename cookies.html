Cookie & Storage Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Zero Invasive Tracking

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and HTML5 Local Storage are small text files or key-value entries placed in your browser when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over a period of time.

2. How ViceCityHub Uses Storage

ViceCityHub.eu operates with a strict Zero Third-Party Tracking policy:

  • Strictly Necessary Technical Storage: Used by our infrastructure host (Cloudflare) for DDoS protection, session routing, and security rate-limiting.
  • Client Preference Storage: We use browser localStorage to remember your active category filters and client-side bookmarks. No user-profiling or marketing trackers are used.

3. Third-Party Services

When you click external links (such as Discord server invite links), you leave ViceCityHub.eu and are subject to Discord's respective privacy and cookie practices.

With your consent, ViceCityHub.eu uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to measure anonymous traffic and improve the site. Analytics cookies are disabled by default and only activate if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Advertising storage, ad user data, and ad personalization remain denied. Your choice is saved in your browser and can be reset by clearing site data.

4. How to Control or Delete Cookies

You can delete or block all cookies through your web browser settings at any time. Doing so will not hinder your ability to browse ViceCityHub.eu.
