1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and HTML5 Local Storage are small text files or key-value entries placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember user preferences, measure audience engagement, and display relevant advertisements.

2. Categories of Cookies & Storage Used on ViceCityHub.eu

  • Strictly Necessary & Security Cookies (Always Active): Used by our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) and authentication backend for DDoS protection, bot verification (Cloudflare Turnstile), session authentication (vicecityhub_session), and security rate-limiting.
  • Functional & Preference Storage (localStorage): We use browser localStorage to save your cookie consent state (vch_cookie_consent), bookmarked posts, and interface preferences.
  • Analytics & Performance Cookies (Consent-Based): With your consent, we use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics (_ga, _gid) to collect aggregated, anonymized insights on page views, traffic sources, and site performance.
  • Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies (Consent-Based): We partner with Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-3729233023466811) to fund and maintain our community platform. Google and its certified advertising partners may set advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie, IDE, __gads, __gpi) to measure ad impressions, prevent click fraud, limit how many times you see an ad, and serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

3. Managing Ad Personalization & Cookies

You can control, restrict, or opt out of personalized advertising cookies at any time directly through Google Ads Settings or your browser preferences.

4. Third-Party Opt-Out & Browser Controls

In addition to our on-site consent controls, you can manage or opt out of personalized advertising cookies across the web through:

  • Google Ads Settings: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Google Partner Sites Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA): https://www.youronlinechoices.eu
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (US DAA): https://www.aboutads.info/choices

You can also delete or block cookies at any time via your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, Opera). For more details on how we handle personal data globally, please read our Global Privacy Policy.
