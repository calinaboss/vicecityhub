Digital Services Act Compliance

Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 Notice • Single Point of Contact

1. Single Point of Contact for Authorities & Users

Pursuant to Articles 11 and 12 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (the Digital Services Act), the single point of contact for communications from Member States authorities, the European Commission, the European Board for Digital Services, and recipients of our services is:

Official Contact Channel: legal@vicecityhub.eu or via our dedicated Report Content Form.

Official communications may be submitted in English or Italian.

2. Content Moderation & Illegal Content Takedown

ViceCityHub.eu operates a prompt Notice and Action mechanism. In accordance with Article 16 of the DSA, upon receiving an substantiated notice of illegal content (including unauthorized distribution of copyrighted material, phishing Discord links, harassment, or malicious scripts), our team acts expeditiously to review and remove the infringing content.

3. Statements of Reasons & Complaint Handling

Where content is restricted or removed, affected users receive a clear statement explaining the decision, citing the specific guideline breached, and instructions on how to contest the decision if they believe the action was taken in error.

4. Average Monthly Active Recipients

ViceCityHub.eu calculates that its average monthly active recipients in the European Union remain significantly below the threshold of 45 million users, classifying this service as a small online platform under the DSA.
