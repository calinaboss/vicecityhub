Discord Listing Rules

Every server submitted to the directory is verified by our moderators before publication and must follow these rules:

  • 1. Permanent Invite Links Only

    Set your Discord invite link to Never Expire before submitting so players can always join.

  • 2. Official Discord Domains Only

    Redirect URLs, URL shorteners (bit.ly), and custom domains are rejected. Only discord.gg and discord.com/invite pass validation.

  • 3. GTA VI & Gaming Focus

    Servers must be related to Grand Theft Auto VI, Vice City Roleplay, Heists, Modding, or gaming communities.

  • 4. No NSFW, Scams, or Paid Cheats

    Servers selling unauthorized menus, account recovery scams, or violating Discord ToS are permanently blacklisted.
