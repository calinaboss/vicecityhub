About ViceCityHub & Contact Us

Built by Gamers, for Gamers • Editorial Standards & Transparency

1. Our Editorial Mission

ViceCityHub.eu was founded out of genuine passion for Grand Theft Auto VI and the Rockstar Games community. As players explore the neon avenues of Vice City, the wetlands of Grassrivers, the Florida Keys of Leonida, and Port Gellhorn, our mission is to provide a fast, well-moderated, and resource-rich portal where players can discuss news, reference verified PS5 and Xbox Series X/S cheat codes, explore an interactive map atlas, and connect with legitimate Discord communities.

2. Editorial & Community Moderation Standards

  • Human Moderation & Safety: Every submitted Discord server and community discussion is subject to anti-spam filtering and active moderation to prevent phishing, toxic behavior, and fake leaks.
  • Original Guides & Resources: Our interactive Leonida map markers, controller cheat guides, and FAQ resources are curated and verified by experienced Grand Theft Auto community contributors.
  • Global Privacy & Security: Hosted on Cloudflare's global edge infrastructure with strict Content Security Policy (CSP) headers and Google Consent Mode v2 compliance.

3. Advertising & Funding Transparency

To keep ViceCityHub.eu 100% free for all players worldwide and cover server, database, and moderation costs, we display non-intrusive advertisements powered by Google AdSense. Advertisements are clearly distinguished from editorial and community content. You can review our Global Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy or adjust your ad personalization settings at any time.
