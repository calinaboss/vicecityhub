5 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about ViceCityHub.eu & map.vicecityhub.eu

24-Hour Response Time Promise: All emails to info@vicecityhub.eu and moderation reports are answered within 24 hours.

1. How do I submit my GTA VI Discord server?

Go to our dedicated Share Your Discord Server page. Enter your server name, select a category (Roleplay, Heists & LFG, Modding, or General), paste an official permanent https://discord.gg/... invite link, and publish it directly to the directory.

2. How do GTA 6 cheat codes work on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S?

In GTA 6, cheats are triggered via the in-game phone using D-pad sequences (there are no 1-999 numbers to dial). Press Up on the D-pad to pull out your phone, hold L1 + press R1 (PS5) or hold LB + press RB (Xbox Series X/S), and enter the D-pad sequence listed on our Cheat Codes Guide.

3. What is your response time for support, verifications, and reports?

We maintain a strict 24-Hour Response Time Promise. Every Discord verification request, bug report, or EU DSA takedown notice sent via Report Content or emailed to info@vicecityhub.eu is reviewed by a human administrator within 24 hours.

4. Is ViceCityHub 100% free to use?

Yes, 100% free! Browsing the interactive Leonida map at map.vicecityhub.eu, posting community discussions, and listing your Discord server require no paid tiers or subscriptions.

5. Is ViceCityHub an official Rockstar Games website?

No. ViceCityHub.eu is an independent community hub built by passionate GTA fans. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc.
