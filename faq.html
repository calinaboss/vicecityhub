Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ViceCityHub.eu

How do I submit my GTA VI Discord server?

Simply click the Submit Discord button on the homepage or in the Discord Servers section. Enter your server name, select a category (such as Roleplay, Italian Community, or Heists), paste a valid https://discord.gg/... invite link, and write a brief description.

Is ViceCityHub free to use?

Yes, 100% free! There are no paid tiers or subscription fees to post discussions or list your community Discord server.

Is ViceCityHub an official Rockstar Games website?

No. ViceCityHub.eu is an independent community project run by passionate fans of the GTA series. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc.

Why was a post or Discord link removed?

Links or posts that violate our Terms of Service (such as expired or invalid invites, phishing links, hate speech, or real-money trading scams) are removed immediately by moderators to keep the community safe.
